S2: analysis of the faculty TaskVoCSI_v2 results

- research/S2_analysis/: analysis script, tables T1-T7 and figures F1-F6 built from
  the two S1 full runs (read-only), console record.
- research/S2_faculty_results.md: interpretation: effect sizes and statistical
  validity, where the gain comes from, sweeps, calibrator and learned-model quality,
  limitations, implications for S3-S7.
- research/checkpoints/S2_checkpoint.md, research/logs/S2/, README stage status.

Main finding: CTVC-DP beats all five baselines significantly (+1.5 % digits,
+3.9 % surrogate); the gain vanishes when every user is served (K = 4), which our side
track always does; the risk penalty has no measurable effect; the trained GRU matches
persistence.
