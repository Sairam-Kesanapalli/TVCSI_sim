S0: research workspace, checkpoint and unmodified faculty code

Stage S0 of the staged integration of TVCSI_sim with the faculty's TaskVoCSI_v2.

- research/README.md: set-up on a new computer, stage plan S0-S7, rules, commit
  convention, model allocation.
- research/checkpoints/S0_checkpoint.md: what S0 did and verified, decisions, next stage.
- research/logs/S0/: S0 evidence (original state, backups, copies, environments, tests,
  integrity check).
- research/manifests/: SHA-256 of every original file (TVCSI_sim at Phase 2, faculty code).
- research/tools/make_manifest.py: write/verify such manifests.
- research/faculty_TaskVoCSI_v2/: the faculty implementation, unmodified (byte-identical
  to its manifest); main-track source for S1.
