## 1. In one paragraph (beginner version)
We ran the final experiments with everything from S3–S6 switched on: the learned value estimate,
the trained codec, the five faculty baselines and the risk-aware variant. That is 700 simulations
over all five sweeps, 10 test seeds each. **The Proposed scheme beats the best of eight simple
baselines at 28 of 29 operating points.** The only exception is "unlimited feedback", where everyone
ties, as expected. It reaches 98 % of a genie that knows the true channel, and needs 2.4–3.4× fewer
feedback bits than the periodic baseline for the same quality.
